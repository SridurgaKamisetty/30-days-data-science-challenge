Using SQL, I performed RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis on an e-commerce dataset to understand customer purchasing behavior. I calculated customer recency, order frequency, and total spending using SQL aggregation functions and segmented customers into High-Value, At-Risk, and Other categories using CASE WHEN logic.

Out of 401 customers:

• High-Value Customers: 9
• At-Risk Customers: 167
• Others: 225

This analysis helps identify valuable customers who contribute significantly to revenue and customers who may require retention strategies. Since the dataset covers a limited transaction period, the segmentation should be considered exploratory rather than a final customer classification.
