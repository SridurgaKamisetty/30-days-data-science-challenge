## Day 16 — Power BI Road Accident Analysis

**Dataset:** India Road Accidents (20,000 rows, 23 columns)
**Question:** Which cause leads to most casualties?

**Causes analyzed:**
- Weather conditions
- Drunk driving
- Overspeeding
- Poor road condition
- Distraction

**Dashboard features:**
- Accidents by Year (Bar Chart)
- Casualties by Cause (Bar Chart)
- Accidents by Day of Week (Line Chart)
- Weather & Traffic Density Slicers

**Final Insight:** Multiple causes contribute 
to road accidents — weather, drunk driving 
and overspeeding are top factors!

**Tool:** Power BI Desktop
**Features:** Slicers, Bar Chart, 
Line Chart, Custom Background
