Break-Even Analysis:

Profit per Unit: $7.56
Assumed Fixed Cost: ₹50,000

The business must sell approximately 6,614 units to cover all costs and reach profitability.
Profit per unit was calculated using total profit and quantity sold.
Discount impact was indirectly considered through the profit data.
