## Day 13 — SQL Cohort Retention Analysis

**Dataset:** Customer Orders (25 rows)
**Question:** How many month-1 customers 
return in month 3?

**Answer:**
- January cohort size: 5 customers
- Returned in March: 2 customers
- Retention rate: 40%
- 60% customers lost by month 3!

**Tool:** SQL (MySQL)
**Key concepts:** Cohort analysis, MIN(),
GROUP BY, JOIN, subquery
