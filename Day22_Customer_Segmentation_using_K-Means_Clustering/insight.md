The K-Means clustering model identified 5 distinct customer personas based on annual income and spending behavior.

1. Premium Customers (High Income, High Spending)
2. Careful Customers (High Income, Low Spending)
3. Impulsive Customers (Low Income, High Spending)
4. Budget Customers (Low Income, Low Spending)
5. Average Customers (Medium Income, Medium Spending)

These customer segments can help businesses create targeted marketing strategies and improve customer engagement.
